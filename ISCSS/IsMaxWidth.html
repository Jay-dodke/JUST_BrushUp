CSS Max-Width

Max-width keeps an element from growing too wide on large screens.

Fixed Width (width: 500px)

Always 500px, even on small screens.

width: 500px;

Max-Width (max-width: 500px)

Shrinks on small screens, stops at 500px on big screens.

max-width: 500px; width: 100%;

Max-Width + Center

Common layout pattern for readable content.

max-width: 600px; margin: 0 auto;
