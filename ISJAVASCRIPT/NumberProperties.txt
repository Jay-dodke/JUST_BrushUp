JavaScript Number Properties (simple notes)

1) Number.MAX_VALUE
Largest possible number value.

2) Number.MIN_VALUE
Smallest positive number value.

3) Number.EPSILON
Difference between 1 and the smallest value greater than 1.

4) Safe Integer Range
MAX_SAFE_INTEGER and MIN_SAFE_INTEGER define safe exact integer bounds.

5) Static Check Methods
Number.isFinite(), Number.isInteger(), Number.isSafeInteger(), Number.isNaN()
