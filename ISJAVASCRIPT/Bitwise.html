JavaScript Bitwise Operators

Bitwise operators work on 32-bit integer values. They are useful for masks, flags, and fast low-level operations.

AND (&), OR (|), XOR (^)


5 & 1;  // 1
5 | 1;  // 5
5 ^ 1;  // 4
