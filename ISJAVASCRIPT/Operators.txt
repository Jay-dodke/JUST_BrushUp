JavaScript Operators (summary based on W3Schools)

1) Arithmetic operators
Use +, -, *, /, %, **, ++, and -- to perform math.

2) Assignment operators
Use = to assign values, and +=, -=, *=, /= to update values quickly.

3) Comparison operators
Use == or === to compare values, and !=, !==, >, <, >=, <= to compare numbers or strings.

4) Real life idea
Operators combine to build real scenarios, like calculating totals, discounts,
shipping, or checking thresholds.
