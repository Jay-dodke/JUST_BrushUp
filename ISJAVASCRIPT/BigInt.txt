JavaScript BigInt (simple notes)

1) Why BigInt
Use BigInt when integers are larger than Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER.

2) Create BigInt
Add n suffix: 123n
Or use BigInt("123")

3) BigInt Arithmetic
Works with +, -, *, /, %, ** on BigInt values.
Division truncates decimals.

4) Mixing Types
Cannot directly mix BigInt and Number in arithmetic.
Convert first using BigInt() or Number().

5) Comparisons
Loose comparison can match Number values.
Strict comparison checks type, so BigInt !== Number.
