JavaScript Loops (simple notes based on W3Schools)

1) for loop
Use for when you know how many times to repeat.
Example: add numbers 1 to 5.

2) while loop
Use while when you repeat until a condition changes.
Example: count down from 3 to 1.

3) break
Stop a loop early.
Example: stop at 4.

4) continue
Skip one step.
Example: skip 3.

5) control flow
Use break and continue to control the loop.
Example: skip 2, stop at 4.
