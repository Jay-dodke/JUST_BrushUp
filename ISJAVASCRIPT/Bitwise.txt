JavaScript Bitwise (simple notes)

1) Bitwise AND (&)
Keeps bit as 1 only when both bits are 1.

2) Bitwise OR (|)
Keeps bit as 1 when either bit is 1.

3) Bitwise XOR (^)
Keeps bit as 1 when bits are different.

4) Bitwise NOT (~)
Flips bits. ~x is same as -(x + 1).

5) Shifts
<< shifts left (multiply by powers of 2 in many cases)
>> shifts right with sign
>>> shifts right without sign

6) Bit Masks
Useful for flags and permission systems.
